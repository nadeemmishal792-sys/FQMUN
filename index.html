03 / DELEGATE PREPARATION

Prepare to debate.

Everything you need to arrive prepared for an online diplomatic floor.

01

Research

Understand your assigned country or PNA personality, the agenda, relevant policies, UN resolutions and credible legal sources.

02

Prepare your speech

Build a clear 60–90 second opening speech. Know your position, priorities and the solutions you want the committee to consider.

03

Master the floor

Prepare POIs, rebuttals and operative clauses. Learn the Rules of Procedure and stay diplomatic even when debate gets intense.

04

Be online-ready

Use a stable connection, join early, keep your microphone muted when not speaking and keep your research and notes ready.

05

For PNA delegates

Research your selected Pakistani political personality and prepare parliamentary arguments, policy positions and responses.

06

Be professional

Respect the chair, fellow delegates and the format of the conference. Strong diplomacy is persuasive, prepared and respectful.

07 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is FQMUN?

FQMUN is an online Model United Nations conference taking place from 26–28 August 2026 with PNA, UNSC and UNHRC.

Is FQMUN online?

Yes. The conference is conducted virtually. Joining instructions will be provided to registered delegates.

What are the registration fees?

Individual Delegate: PKR 500 per person. Delegation: 5 delegates for PKR 500 total, which is PKR 100 per delegate. Observer: PKR 500 per observer.

How do I register?

Complete the registration form, select your committee and country preference, pay the applicable fee and upload a clear payment screenshot.

Can I choose my country?

You can submit a country preference. Final allocation is subject to the conference's allocation process.

What if I choose PNA?

PNA delegates also select a preferred Pakistani political personality from the available list.

Do I need previous MUN experience?

No. Beginners are welcome. The preparation guide above is designed to help first-time delegates get started.

What should I prepare?

Research, an opening speech, POIs and rebuttals, and draft-resolution ideas. Follow any additional instructions issued by the FQMUN team.
