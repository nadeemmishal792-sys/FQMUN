07 / FAQ
Is FQMUN completely online?

Yes. FQMUN 2026 is a virtual Model United Nations conference running from 26–28 August.

What is the registration fee?

The registration fee is PKR 500. Payment instructions are shown above and the receipt screenshot is required during registration.

When will my registration be approved?

Registrations are reviewed after payment verification. Keep your registration ID and payment receipt safe.

What should I prepare before committee?

Research your country or personality, understand the agenda, prepare an opening speech and come ready to negotiate.

Where will the online meeting link be shared?

Conference access details and announcements will be shared with registered delegates through the official communication channels.
