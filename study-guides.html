OFFICIAL STUDY GUIDE ANNOUNCEMENT · 2026

Study Guides are now available.

FQMUN has released general preparation guides for all three committees. These are not agenda-specific: they explain how to research, prepare a position paper, write speeches, use sources, participate in caucuses, draft working papers/resolutions and prepare for conference procedure.

WHAT EVERY DELEGATE SHOULD PREPARE

Research

Committee mandate, assigned country/role, national interests, previous UN action, laws/treaties, stakeholders and credible evidence.

Position Paper

Header → introduction → national/role position → previous action → proposed solutions → conclusion, using clear evidence and citations.

Speeches

Prepare a 60–90 second GSL, focused moderated-caucus speeches, POIs and concise diplomatic rebuttals.

Drafting

Learn preambulatory and operative clauses, realistic implementation, responsible actors, monitoring and measurable outcomes.

POSITION PAPER SAMPLE PATTERN

Recommended structure

1. Header: Committee, agenda, country/role and delegate if required.

2. Introduction: Define the issue and explain its significance.

3. Position: State the assigned country's or PNA personality's established position, interests and priorities.

4. Previous action: Reference relevant UN resolutions, treaties, government policies, legislation, statements or votes.

5. Solutions: Present 2–4 realistic proposals and explain implementation.

6. Conclusion: Reaffirm the position and identify the cooperation required.

Sample opening: “The delegation of [Country] recognizes that [issue] requires coordinated action while respecting [principle/national interest].”

HARVARD-STYLE RESEARCH

For UNSC & UNHRC preparation

Use a Harvard-style research approach: establish the problem and context; identify stakeholders; examine the legal and institutional framework; evaluate competing policy options; support claims with reliable sources; and finish with practical recommendations. Prioritize primary sources such as UN documents, treaties, official government documents and legislation, then use reputable academic and institutional research for analysis.

PNA-SPECIFIC PREPARATION

Pakistan National Assembly

The PNA guide focuses on parliamentary debate, political-personality research, policy positions, coalition building, amendments, floor speeches, rebuttals and practical legislation. Delegates should understand their selected personality's political record and distinguish documented facts from claims or political rhetoric.

Download PNA Study Guide (PDF)

UNSC

United Nations Security Council

The UNSC guide focuses on the Council mandate, peace and security, Chapter VI/VII concepts, state interests, sanctions and enforcement tools, humanitarian consequences, veto dynamics, regional organizations and realistic Council action. It is a general guide and is not tied to a single agenda.

Download UNSC Study Guide (PDF)

UNHRC

United Nations Human Rights Council

The UNHRC guide focuses on human-rights standards, treaty obligations, Universal Periodic Review, Special Procedures, fact-finding and accountability mechanisms, vulnerable groups, equality, social justice and evidence-based recommendations. It is a general guide and is not tied to a single agenda.
