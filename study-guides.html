HARVARD-STYLE RESEARCH

Evidence before rhetoric

Use author–date research notes and a reference list containing sources actually consulted. Prefer UN documents, treaties, constitutions, legislation, official government statements and parliamentary records. Use reputable academic and institutional research for analysis. Record publication and access dates for web sources.

POSITION PAPER TEMPLATE

Recommended structure

  1. Committee, agenda, country/personality and delegate
  2. Introduction and significance
  3. Assigned national or personality position
  4. Previous UN, government or parliamentary action
  5. Legal and institutional framework
  6. Gaps and challenges
  7. Three to five realistic solutions with implementation
  8. Conclusion and cooperation strategy
  9. Harvard-style reference list

FINAL CHECK

Before conference, have your position paper, GSL, caucus speeches, POIs, rebuttals, draft clauses, source sheet and conference rules ready. Keep research accessible offline and verify serious factual claims before using them on the floor.
